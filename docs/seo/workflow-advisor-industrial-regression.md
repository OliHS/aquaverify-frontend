# Workflow Advisor industrial regression

## Test case

The industrial fixture represents process water with custom software, email, spreadsheets, custody and CoA stages, mixed laboratory model, qualitative and quantitative result, 1 mL volume, operational screening, other methodological reference, and water uses including drinking water, process water and reclaimed water.

## Expected report behavior

- Shows `Informe de diagnóstico` and `Agua de proceso industrial`.
- Localizes priority problems as process, laboratory and quality coordination, critical process point control, and external laboratory coordination.
- Marks the analytical route as pending technical review.
- Shows INDICA, ENUMERA and PLAQUE / ISO-EPA only as families to review, not as closed product recommendations.
- Expands missing information into a technical checklist.
- Includes related resources with descriptions and localized URLs.
