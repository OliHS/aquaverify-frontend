# AquaTools Free Editorial Review

## Editorial Date

Reviewed locally on 2026-06-22.

## Claims Policy

The public copy intentionally avoids:

- regulatory pass/fail decisions
- ISO, EPA or authority acceptance claims
- universal CFU countability ranges
- hardness classifications as legal criteria
- saved-record, history, ELN, LIMS or collaboration claims for the free browser tools
- unsupported "coming soon" calculator pages

The free calculators are described as educational and workflow aids. Controlled records and review workflows remain positioned in AquaVerify Cloud.

## Localization Notes

All hub and calculator routes have dedicated EN, ES, FR, IT and CA titles, descriptions, UI labels, privacy copy, disclaimers, FAQ and page body sections.

Some scientific symbols remain language-neutral by design:

- CFU/UFC
- RPD
- RPM/RCF
- CaCO3
- NO3/NO3-N and related nutrient forms

## Source Notes

The page source references the AquaVerify Cloud AquaTools core and AquaTools Free v1 acceptance vectors. Public pages do not reproduce external regulatory thresholds or legal limits.
