# Downloadable checklist — Water risk management in facilities: Legionella, terminal points and traceability

**Audience:** Facility managers, hotels, resorts, spas, maintenance providers, labs and risk teams
**Region:** Spain / European Union / Global buildings

## Checklist

- [ ] Create an asset inventory with unique point IDs.
- [ ] Classify aerosol-generating and high-risk points.
- [ ] Define sampling frequency and responsible supplier or team.
- [ ] Record maintenance, temperature and operational context.
- [ ] Link laboratory reports to asset records and actions.
- [ ] Track deviations, corrective actions and follow-up samples.
- [ ] Prepare inspection-ready evidence by site and asset.
