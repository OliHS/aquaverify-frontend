**Audience:** Laboratories, quality teams, utilities, distributors and OEM partners
**Region:** Global

## Checklist

- [ ] Define the analytical question before selecting a product.
- [ ] List sample matrices, volumes, frequency and operational locations.
- [ ] Decide whether the output must be quantitative or presence/absence.
- [ ] Identify whether ISO/EPA-aligned method preparation is required.
- [ ] Document lot, operator, control and review requirements.
- [ ] Decide whether CoA, customer portal or audit trail are required.
- [ ] Map the selected product to a sector page and commercial next step.
