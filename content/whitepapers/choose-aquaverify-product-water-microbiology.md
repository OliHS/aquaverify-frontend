<h2 id="executive-summary">Executive summary</h2>

- Start with the decision you need: quantify, screen, document a reference workflow, standardize laboratory consumables or digitize evidence.
- Use ENUMERA when a quantitative microbiological result is needed and the output must be interpreted, trended or reported.
- Use INDICA when the operational question is presence/absence, rapid verification or routine screening.
- Use ISO/EPA Kits and Lab Essentials when the priority is method-oriented execution, consistency and laboratory control.
- Use AquaVerify Cloud when the result must be connected to sample, lot, operator, CoA, customer portal or audit trail.

<h2 id="technical-context">Technical context</h2>

### Start from the decision, not from the product

A product selection process is stronger when the team first defines the decision to be made. A municipal operator may need a trendable count, a food plant may need rapid release confidence, and a distributor may need a portfolio that covers both routine screening and laboratory workflows. AquaVerify families are designed to map those decisions to the right operational path.

### A practical decision rule

If the question is “how many?”, start with ENUMERA. If the question is “present or absent?”, start with INDICA. If the question is “which recognized method workflow should we prepare?”, review ISO/EPA Kits. If the question is “how do we reduce variability in daily lab execution?”, review Lab Essentials. If the question is “how do we prove what happened?”, connect the workflow to AquaVerify Cloud.

### What changes when Cloud is added

Digital traceability changes the conversation from a standalone result to a documented chain of evidence. Sample, matrix, location, method, product lot, operator, review, CoA and customer delivery become part of the same operational record.

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

### Can one AquaVerify family cover every use case?

No. The right family depends on whether the decision requires enumeration, screening, method-oriented execution, laboratory consistency or digital traceability.

### Is AquaVerify Cloud required to use the products?

Products can be positioned independently, but Cloud becomes important when samples, lots, users, CoA and audit trail must be connected.

### Can distributors use this guide?

Yes. It provides a simple way to qualify opportunities by matrix, urgency, method expectation and buyer profile.
