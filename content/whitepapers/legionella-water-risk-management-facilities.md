<h2 id="executive-summary">Executive summary</h2>

- Facilities need water risk management that covers assets, points, suppliers, laboratories, corrective actions and inspections.
- Legionella control depends on a plan, not on isolated test results; sampling and actions must be linked to asset context.
- Terminal points, DHW loops, deposits, spas, pools and aerosol-generating equipment require different records and response rules.
- Digital traceability helps multi-site teams maintain evidence across maintenance, laboratory reports and follow-up actions.
- AquaVerify can support routine water evidence workflows, while Legionella-specific analytical requirements remain method and laboratory dependent.

<h2 id="technical-context">Technical context</h2>

### Asset-based water risk

A building is a network of assets: hot water, cold water, deposits, terminal points, cooling or aerosol equipment, pools and spas. Each asset needs its own risk context, sampling logic and corrective-action route.

### Legionella evidence is a workflow

A laboratory result is only one part of the file. A stronger record includes asset ID, point, temperature or operational data, maintenance action, supplier evidence, laboratory report and follow-up decision.

### Multi-site control

Hotels, resorts, campuses and operators with multiple locations need comparable records. A standard digital workflow reduces inconsistent forms and helps inspections focus on evidence rather than reconstruction.

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

### Does AquaVerify replace a Legionella laboratory method?

No. Legionella-specific testing depends on the selected method and laboratory. AquaVerify supports water evidence workflows and traceability around the program.

### Who benefits most from a digital record?

Multi-site operators, hotels, spas and facilities with multiple suppliers benefit from consistent asset-level records.

### Can this support inspections?

Yes. A connected file helps demonstrate plan, sampling, reports, actions and follow-up without reconstructing evidence manually.
